Under The Hood

How Our E-Ink Physics Engine Works

1. Electrophoretic Drift Velocity (Stokes' Law)

Every microcapsule contains positively charged white Titanium Dioxide (TiO₂) spheres and negatively charged black Carbon Black spheres suspended in clear hydrocarbon oil. When an electric field E is applied across the top and bottom electrodes, particles reach terminal drift velocity almost instantaneously:

v = (q · E) / (6 · π · η(T) · r)

2. Temperature & Viscosity (Arrhenius Scaling)

Lowering ambient temperature exponentially increases carrier oil viscosity η(T) = η₀ · exp(k · (22 − T)). At −15°C, oil becomes ~6× thicker, reducing drift speed so particles fail to reach the top electrode during a single pulse—producing realistic ghosting.

3. Multi-Phase Anti-Ghosting Waveform

Real E-Ink controllers solve ghosting and achieve accurate grayscale using a 3-phase voltage sequence: Phase 1 drives all particles to full Black (+15V) to erase prior positions; Phase 2 flushes them to full White (−15V) to establish a clean baseline; Phase 3 applies a calibrated pulse duration to position the exact ratio of white and black spheres near the viewing surface.

4. Honest Engineering Simplifications

  • Each of the 64 pixels runs a live 2D cross-section simulation (28 pigment spheres per capsule, 1,792 total simulated particles) rather than full 3D fluid dynamics.
  • Optical reflectance is computed directly from the vertical positions of white vs. black particles in the upper optical zone of each capsule.
  • Microcapsules are rendered in an ordered row cross-section; in real electrophoretic film, capsules are packed in a random monolayer.
